1What stays on your devices

These are stored only on your computer (in Chrome's extension storage) or on your iPhone, and are never sent to us:

  • Your settings, including the app's language and your daily reminder time.
  • Your AI service keys, for example a Google Gemini key. On the iPhone, a key you choose to share from the extension is kept in the phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain).
  • The words you save, their meanings, your quizzes and flashcards, and your review progress.
  • Downloaded offline models (voices, translation and in-browser AI), cached in your browser.

You can delete all of it at any time: remove the extension, use Remove everything from this phone in SuperHap's Settings, or delete the app.

2What goes to an AI service, and when

When you hover a word, ask for a translation, explanation or recap, chat with Hap, or play a natural voice, the text needed for that request is sent to the AI service you chose in Setup. That's usually the subtitle line, the word, the show's title and a few recent lines for context, or your chat message. When you talk to Hap on the iPhone, your recording is sent to Google Gemini so it can be written down as text.

The request goes directly from your device to that service, with your own key. It doesn't pass through us, and we can't see it.

3Reading subtitles

On Netflix, Prime Video and YouTube, the extension reads the subtitles shown on the page, and the subtitle tracks the episode offers, so it can explain and translate them. They're kept in the browser's memory while you watch, and only sent anywhere as part of an AI request you make (section 2) or to your own phone (section 4). The extension doesn't run on any other website.

4Syncing with your phone

SubLink AI and SuperHap talk to each other directly over your home Wi-Fi, after you pair them once with a code. Your words, review progress and live subtitle lines go straight between your computer and your phone, never through the internet or us.

Your phone only answers the computer it was paired with, which proves itself with a key made when you paired. This traffic stays inside your home network, but apart from your AI key it isn't encrypted, so only sync on a Wi-Fi network you trust. If you turn on Let my phone use my Gemini key, your key is encrypted with the pairing key on the way and stored in your phone's secure storage.

If your phone's Wi-Fi address changes, the extension may check the other devices on your home network to find it again. Only your own paired phone can answer that check.

5Downloads

If you choose an offline voice, offline translation or in-browser AI, its model files are downloaded once from Hugging Face. Like any website, Hugging Face can see your IP address when you download. See the Hugging Face privacy policy.

6Payments

Tips in the extension

Handled by Stripe on Stripe's own page. Stripe receives your card details and email. We only see the amount, your email and your country, never your full card number. Stripe Privacy Policy

Unlocking SuperHap

A purchase through Apple. Apple handles the payment and only tells the app that the purchase was made. Apple Privacy Policy

7iPhone permissions

Microphone only while you tap to talk to Hap or practise pronunciation. The recording goes to Google Gemini to be written down (section 2) and isn't kept.

Local network to sync with SubLink AI on your computer (section 4).

Notifications for your daily review reminder, scheduled on the phone itself.

8What we don't do

  • We don't have accounts, and we don't collect your name or email in the apps.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data.

9Children

The apps aren't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. The apps don't collect personal information from anyone (section 8).

Privacy Policy

For the SubLink AI Chrome extension and the SuperHap iPhone app, made by Alethetic Labs, Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

In short: we don't run a server that collects your data, and you don't need an account. Your words, settings and review progress stay on your own computer and phone. Text is only sent to an AI service when you use one, and it goes straight from your device to the service you picked, using your own key. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you.

1. What stays on your devices

These are stored only on your computer (in Chrome's extension storage) or on your iPhone, and are never sent to us:

  • Your settings, including the app's language and your daily reminder time.
  • Your AI service keys (for example a Google Gemini key). On the iPhone, a key you choose to share from the extension is kept in the phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain).
  • The words you save, their meanings, your quizzes and flashcards, and your review progress.
  • Downloaded offline models (voices, translation and in-browser AI), cached in your browser.

You can delete all of it at any time: remove the extension, or use "Remove everything from this phone" in SuperHap's Settings, or delete the app.

2. What is sent to an AI service, and when

When you hover a word, ask for a translation, explanation or recap, chat with Hap, or play a natural voice, SubLink AI or SuperHap sends the text needed for that request to the AI service you chose in Setup. That is usually the subtitle line, the word, the show's title and recent lines for context, or your chat message. With "talk to Hap" on the iPhone, your recorded voice is sent to Google Gemini so it can be written down as text.

The request goes directly from your device to that service with your own key. It does not pass through us, and we can't see it. The services you can choose are:

On a free Gemini key, Google may use what you send to improve its products. If that matters to you, use a paid key or one of the on-device options.

3. Reading subtitles

On Netflix, Prime Video and YouTube, the extension reads the subtitles shown on the page, and the subtitle tracks the episode offers, so it can explain and translate them. Subtitles are kept in the browser's memory while you watch. They are only sent anywhere as part of an AI request you make (section 2), or to your own phone (section 4). The extension doesn't run on other websites.

4. Syncing with your phone

SubLink AI and SuperHap talk to each other directly over your home Wi-Fi after you pair them with a one-time code. Your words, review progress and live subtitle lines go straight from your computer to your phone and back, and never through the internet or us. Your phone only answers the computer it was paired with, which proves itself with a key made when you paired. This traffic stays inside your home network, but apart from your AI key it isn't encrypted, so only sync on a Wi-Fi network you trust. If you turn on "Let my phone use my Gemini key", your key is encrypted with the pairing key on the way and stored in your phone's secure storage.

To find your phone again when its Wi-Fi address changes, the extension may check the other devices on your home network for SuperHap. It only sends a check that your own paired phone can answer.

5. Downloads

If you choose an offline voice, offline translation or in-browser AI, the model files are downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) or the model's publisher. Like any website, they can see your IP address when you download. See the Hugging Face privacy policy.

6. Payments

  • Tips in the extension are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own page. Stripe receives your card details and email. We only receive the amount, your email and your country, and never your full card number. See stripe.com/privacy.
  • Unlocking SuperHap is a purchase through Apple. Apple handles the payment and tells the app only that the purchase was made. See Apple's privacy policy.

7. iPhone permissions

  • Microphone: only while you hold "talk to Hap" or practise pronunciation. The recording is sent to Google Gemini to be written down (section 2) and isn't kept.
  • Local network: to sync with SubLink AI on your computer (section 4).
  • Notifications: your daily review reminder, scheduled on the phone itself.

8. What we don't do

  • We don't have accounts, and we don't collect your name or email in the apps.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data.

9. Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. The apps themselves don't collect personal information from anyone (section 8).
